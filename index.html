Michigan Wolverbots · RL & Controls Lead

Humanoid sim-to-real: kinematic modeling & PPO policy training

Challenge
Bridging the sim-to-real gap for a bipedal RoboCup humanoid under tight mass and contact-physics constraints.
Approach
Exported Onshape CAD to USD/URDF, verified per-link masses, and stabilized MuJoCo contact physics. Trained velocity-tracking PPO policies with multi-objective reward shaping. Currently training on a simplified robot model before scaling up to the full 20-DOF humanoid.
Outcome
Debugged reward plateaus and inverted-knee gaits; built physical joint-verification workflows for Pypot hardware bring-up.
  • Reinforcement learning

  • URDF / USD
  • Sim-to-real

Field Robotics Group (FRoG) · Prof. Skinner

Real-time side-scan sonar simulation in NVIDIA Isaac Sim

Challenge
At-sea acoustic sensor testing is slow and expensive; high-fidelity synthetic environments can replace much of it.
Approach
Independently built the side-scan sonar module for OceanSim using custom CUDA ray-tracing kernels, PhysX, Warp, and OpenUSD scenes.
Outcome
Qualitatively validated acoustic returns against real shipwreck survey data. Recognized with a CRA National Honorable Mention.
Publication
Separate from the sonar module: I built assets and synthetic data generation (SDG) for randomized scenes used in the object-detection work of “Towards Scaling Marine Perception with Synthetic Data” (IEEE OCEANS 2026, co-author).
  • CUDA
